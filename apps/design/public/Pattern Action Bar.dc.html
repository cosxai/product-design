墨色浮动栏在页面底部居中。它只放当前页面最常用的动作；选中内容或进入模式时，栏里的内容整体换掉。An ink bar floats at the bottom centre. It carries only the page’s most used actions; selecting items or entering a mode swaps its contents as a whole.

三种状态Three states

空闲Idle

页面最常用的 3–5 个动作，例如新建、上传、选择、评论。The page’s 3 to 5 most used actions, such as New, Upload, Select, Comment.

已选择Selection

选中至少一项时出现：数量、分享、移动、删除、取消（Esc）。不放下载、星标这类单项动作。Appears with one or more items selected: the count, Share, Move, Delete, Cancel (Esc). No per-item actions such as Download or Star.

模式Mode

评论、翻译、演示这类模式是操作栏的一个状态：栏里写明当前模式，并带“完成”。Comment, translate and present are states of the bar: it names the mode and carries Done.

响应式Responsive

空间变窄时按顺序收起：先去掉快捷键，再去掉文字只留图标，最后收成左缘把手。默认状态由用户偏好决定。As space narrows it folds in order: shortcuts go first, then labels leaving icons, then it folds into a handle on the left edge. The default follows the user’s preference.

规则Rules

克制Sparing一个状态最多 5 个动作。其余进“更多”，或者只用快捷键和 ⌘K。Five actions per state at most. The rest go under More, or live on shortcuts and ⌘K.
页面注册Registered by the page页面注册自己的动作，离开时自动撤回；没有动作时不显示。Pages register their actions and withdraw them on leave; with none, the bar hides.
可以拖动Draggable左侧抓手拖动，双击复位；位置和收起状态会记住。Drag by the grip, double-click to reset; position and fold state are remembered.
隐藏Hidden签署、演示和全屏查看时隐藏。Hidden while signing, presenting or viewing full screen.
手机Mobile选择状态替换底部标签栏；空闲时收成左缘把手。Selection replaces the bottom tabs; idle folds to the left-edge handle.
