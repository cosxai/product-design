基础 · 动效Foundations · Motion

像纸张落定：一次入场，然后静止Paper settling: one entry, then stillness

动效分两种节奏。状态变化只动颜色、边框和透明度；入场是一次有编排的落定。没有弹跳、没有循环，滚动时不动。Two registers. State changes move colour, border and opacity; entry is one choreographed settle. Nothing bounces or loops, and nothing moves on scroll.

两种节奏Two registers

状态State

悬停、聚焦、切换。颜色 120ms，淡入淡出 180ms，最长 320ms，线性缓动。Hover, focus, toggle. Colour 120ms, fade 180ms, 320ms at most, flat easing.

入场Entry

界面出现时一次落定：行上移 10px，图表块从 .92 放大，柱子从基线长出，标记最后画出。每个元素 ≤ 640ms，间隔 70ms，整段 ≤ 1.4s。When a surface appears: rows rise 10px, chart blocks scale from .92, columns grow from the baseline, the marker draws last. Each element ≤ 640ms, 70ms stagger, whole sequence ≤ 1.4s.

该做与不该做Do and don’t

只动一次Once入场只在界面第一次出现时播放，重新渲染不重播。Entry plays when a surface first appears, not on re-render.
选中项滑动Glide分段控件、标签页的选中项滑到新位置，不闪现。The selected pill in segments and tabs glides; it doesn’t blink.
不做Never弹跳、弹簧、循环动画、视差、随滚动移动。唯一的例外是后台任务的缓慢明暗圆点。Bounce, spring, looping animation, parallax, scroll-linked motion. The one exception is the slow pulse on a running background task.
