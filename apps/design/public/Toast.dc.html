只报告页面之外的结果Report only what happened off the page

墨色提示条出现在右下角。结果能在原位显示时（按钮、行、字段）就不用提示；危险操作用对话框确认，而不是事后提示。An ink notice in the bottom right. When a result can show in place (a button, a row, a field), skip the toast; confirm destructive actions in a dialog, not after the fact.

用法Usage

何时用When结果发生在视线之外：复制到剪贴板、后台移动、另一个页面完成的上传。The result is out of view: copying to the clipboard, a background move, an upload finishing elsewhere.
文案Copy一句过去式陈述：“3 个文件已移到归档。”错误写原因和下一步。One sentence in the past tense: “3 files moved to Archive.” Errors say why and what next.
动作Action最多一个：撤销或重试。带动作时停留 8 秒，鼠标悬停时暂停计时。At most one: Undo or Retry. With an action it stays 8 seconds and pauses on hover.
堆叠Stacking右下角，最多 3 条；同一错误 30 秒内只出现一次。避开操作栏和缩放胶囊。Bottom right, three at most; the same error once per 30 seconds. Keeps clear of the action bar and zoom pill.

无障碍Accessibility

播报Announcements放在 aria-live="polite" 区域；错误用 assertive。In an aria-live="polite" region; errors use assertive.
不抢焦点No focus steal出现时不移动焦点；可以用键盘到达动作按钮。It never takes focus; its action is reachable by keyboard.
