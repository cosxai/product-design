纸、墨和一种黄色Paper, ink and one yellow

纸与亚麻是页面，墨色写所有文字，黄色是唯一的彩色：大面积用浅的 field，小面积用饱和的 accent，永远不用来写字。点击色块可复制变量。Paper and linen are the page, ink writes every letter, and the yellow is the only colour: the pale field for large areas, the saturated accent for small ones, and never for letters. Click a swatch to copy its variable.

材料Materials

浅端四层用于页面与下沉，深端两层用于墨色页。两种灰不能互换：纸上用 grey，墨上用 grey-inverse。Four light steps for the page and sunk areas, two dark steps for the ink page. The two greys never swap: grey on paper, grey-inverse on ink.

黄色The yellow

同一个色相（OKLCH 85），按面积分两档。小面积需要更高饱和度才能和大面积看起来是同一种颜色。One hue, OKLCH 85, split by area. A small area needs more chroma to read as the same colour as a large one.

Field · #FFE3A0区块底色、选中的标签、墨色页上的面板Section grounds, the selected tab, a panel on ink
大面积用。上面的文字用墨色，次要文字用 70% 墨色，不用灰。For large areas. Text on it is ink; secondary text is ink at 70%, never grey.
Accent · #FFD166
标记、图表中的发现、4px 线、状态点The marker, the finding in a chart, 4px rules, status dots
小面积用。墨色页上的标题强调、按钮也用 accent。For small areas. On ink, headline emphasis and the button take the accent too.

墨色页On ink

在墨色上规则反过来：标题强调、按钮这些小面积用 accent；field 只用于墨色页上的面板。墨色页上不画标记色带。On ink the rule flips: headline emphasis and the button are small against the dark and take the accent; the field is only for panels. No marker band on ink.

该做与不该做Do and don’t

该做Do
黄色做底，墨色写字Yellow as ground, ink for type
黄色是底色或标记，文字总是墨色。Yellow is a ground or a mark; type is always ink.
不该做Don’t
黄色的文字Yellow type
不要在纸面上用任何黄色写字。Never set type in any yellow on paper.
该做Do
发布Publish取消Cancel
对比由墨色承担：纸上的主按钮是墨色。Contrast is ink’s job: the primary button on paper is ink.
不该做Don’t
不用渐变、光晕、玻璃、纹理。No gradients, glow, glass or texture.
