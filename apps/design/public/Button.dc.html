用法Usage

一个主按钮One primary每个视图只放一个 Primary；其余用 Secondary 或 Ghost。One Primary per view; the rest are Secondary or Ghost.
动词 + 宾语Verb and object文案写清楚会发生什么：发布登记册、发送 4 条提醒。不用“确定”“提交”。Say what happens: Publish register, Send 4 reminders. Not OK or Submit.
异步结果留在按钮里Keep async results in place进行中锁定宽度并显示转圈；成功在原位确认 1.6 秒；失败在按钮下方写原因并给重试。While busy, lock the width and show a spinner; confirm success in place for 1.6s; on failure explain below the button and offer Retry.
危险操作Destructive actions永久删除用红色按钮，并放在对话框里二次确认。Permanent deletion uses the red button inside a confirming dialog.
Yellow 类型The Yellow variant在任何底色上都填黄色，只用于行内需要处理的快捷操作。Fills yellow on any ground; only for inline actions that need attention.
