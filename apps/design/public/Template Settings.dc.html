结构Structure

菜单Menu按“工作区 / 你”分组，每项带图标；顶部“返回工作区”。Grouped by Workspace and You, each with an icon; Back to workspace on top.
两列Two columns左列标题和一句说明，右列控件；宽度不够时上下排。Title and one line on the left, the control on the right; stacked when narrow.
保存Saving开关立即生效并出提示；表单修改后出现底部保存条，离开前提示。Switches apply with a toast; form edits raise a save bar and warn before leaving.
危险区Danger zone删除、转移所有权放在页面底部单独卡片，输入名称确认。Delete and transfer ownership sit in a separate card at the bottom, confirmed by typing the name.
