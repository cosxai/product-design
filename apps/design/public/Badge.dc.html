状态徽章Badge

只用于产品界面。形状区分紧急程度：填充表示需要人处理，描边表示进行中，圆点表示已知状态。文字始终在场。Product UI only. Shape carries urgency: a fill needs a person, an outline is in progress, a dot is a known state. The wording is always there.

用法Usage

默认外观Default appearance需要处理和错误用填充，进行中用描边，已完成和中性用圆点。只有在一列里需要统一密度时才改 appearance。Attention and error fill, progress outlines, complete and neutral use a dot. Change appearance only to match density in a column.
状态文字Wording写当前状态，而不是动作：“等你处理”，不是“去处理”。Name the state, not the action: Awaiting you, not Review now.
属性不是状态Attributes aren’t states格式、来源、权限这类属性用 Tag（亚麻底），不用状态色。Format, origin and permissions are Tags on linen, not status colours.
只在产品里Product only品牌、营销和演示不出现状态色。Status colours never appear on brand, marketing or decks.

该做与不该做Do and don’t

该做Do
等你处理Awaiting you已交付Delivered
每个徽章都有文字。Every badge has wording.
不该做Don’t
不要只用颜色表示状态。行内只放圆点时，也要保留可读出的文字和悬停说明。Don’t rely on colour alone. A bare dot in a row still needs readable text and a tooltip.

不只靠颜色Not colour alone文字总在；圆点模式在行内保留屏幕阅读器可读的文字。Wording is always present; dot mode keeps screen-reader text in the row.
