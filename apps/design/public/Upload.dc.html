全屏落点Full-screen drop

文件拖进浏览器窗口时，整页盖上一层落点，放到任何位置都能上传。试着把文件拖到这个页面的任意位置，或者点下面的按钮预览。When files enter the browser window, a drop layer covers the page and a drop anywhere uploads. Drag a file anywhere on this page, or preview it below.

预览全屏落点Preview full-screen dropEsc 或点击任意处关闭Esc or click anywhere to close
何时出现When只在拖入的是文件时出现（dataTransfer 含 Files），拖动页面里的文字或卡片不会触发。Only when the drag carries files (dataTransfer has Files); dragging text or cards on the page doesn’t trigger it.
目标Target写明文件会上传到哪里：当前文件夹，或者没有文件夹时的“我的上传”。Name where files will go: the current folder, or My uploads when there isn’t one.
和局部落点With local zones全屏层出现时，页面里的文件夹和局部落点仍然可以接收，悬停在上面时全屏层的文字改为那个文件夹。Folders and local zones still accept under the layer; hovering one changes the layer’s wording to that folder.
层级Layer在对话框之下、页面之上；对话框打开时不出现。Above the page, below dialogs; it never appears while a dialog is open.
离开Leaving拖出窗口、按 Esc 或放下后立即消失，没有延迟。Leaves at once when the drag exits the window, on Esc, or after the drop.
权限Permission只读的页面不显示；显示一行说明“你在这里只能查看”。Read-only pages don’t show it; instead a line says you can only view here.

用法Usage

整个窗口都是落点The whole window accepts文件拖进窗口时，页面上的文件夹、列表和落点同时亮起；只在具体区域上松开时才上传。When files enter the window, folders, lists and zones light up; upload happens only on release over one of them.
上传前检查Check before upload拖入文件夹时先列出将上传的内容，标出不支持的类型和过深的层级，再确认。For folders, list what will upload and flag unsupported types and deep nesting before confirming.
进度Progress每个文件一行：名称、百分比、可取消。批次写“28 / 41 · 1 个失败”。One row per file: name, percentage, cancel. Batches read 28 / 41 · 1 failed.
失败Failures失败的行加浅红底，写原因并提供重试；其他文件继续上传。Failed rows take a red wash with the reason and Retry; the rest keep going.
离开页面Leaving上传中关闭页面会提示；刷新后批次仍在。Closing the page mid-upload asks first; batches survive a refresh.
手机Mobile没有拖放：只显示“选择文件”和“拍照上传”。No drag and drop: Choose files and Take a photo only.

无障碍Accessibility

键盘Keyboard落点里的“选择文件”是真正的按钮；拖放不是唯一方式。Choose files is a real button; drag and drop is never the only way.
播报Announcements进度按 25% 步进播报；完成和失败会读出。Progress is announced in 25% steps; completion and failure are read out.
