01 / Foundations and injection

Product UI uses three materials: paper, ink and one brand colour

The workspace colour is injected through two variables and replaces the COSX yellow. Ink always carries contrast: the primary button, body text and icons stay ink, so any customer colour keeps things readable. The brand colour is for areas (selection, section grounds) and small marks (dots, progress, highlights), never for text.

Injection points

--brand-fieldSelected nav, selected tabs, section grounds
--brand-markDots, progress bars, highlights, attention fills
--ink (not injectable)Primary button, body text, icons
--status-error (not injectable)Errors, overdue, destructive actions

Rules

·Copy is verb and object in sentence case: Publish register, not OK.
·Inline quick actions that change data expand a confirmation in place; buttons in dialogs act directly.
·Buttons and stars inside clickable cards or rows stop propagation and don’t open the item.
·Shortcut hints sit at the end of the button; the width doesn’t change with state.
·Hover deepens one step: no lift, no scale, no shadow. Focus is a 2px ink ring at 3px offset.
