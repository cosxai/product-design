结构Structure

页头Header面包屑到文件名，后接格式和状态；星标在最右。Breadcrumb to the file name, then format and state; the star far right.
画布Canvas亚麻底上的纸面页面；页码和尺寸在页面上方。水印用 7% 墨色。Paper pages on linen; page number and size above. Watermark at 7% ink.
操作栏Action bar下载、分享、评论、演示；评论模式时变为模式状态，带“完成”。Download, Share, Comment, Present; in comment mode it becomes the mode state with Done.
缩放Zoom右下胶囊：−、比例、+、适合页面。Bottom-right pill: minus, percentage, plus, fit page.
评论Comments右侧 340 面板，按未解决 / 已解决分组；点击跳到评论钉。手机上为底部抽屉。A 340 panel grouped by open and resolved; click to jump to the pin. A bottom sheet on phones.
