设计原则Principles

01可追溯Traceable每个数字都有出处，每个动作都有记录。Every figure has a source and every action a record.
02保密Confidential by construction权限写在结构里：客户只看到分享给自己的内容。Access is built into the structure: customers see only what is shared with them.
03负责Accountable对外或不可撤销的操作，由人确认后才执行。Anything that leaves the workspace or can’t be undone waits for a person.
04平实Plain短句，事实写在前面，不用形容词造势。Short sentences, the fact first, no adjectives for effect.
