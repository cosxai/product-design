组件 · 勾选与开关Components · Checkbox and switch

勾选框选择，开关立即生效Checkboxes choose; switches act at once

勾选框用于表单里的选择，提交时才生效；开关用于设置，拨动即生效。两者都有可点击的文字标签。A checkbox is a choice in a form that takes effect on submit; a switch is a setting that takes effect when flipped. Both carry a clickable label.

用哪一个Which one

勾选框Checkbox表单中的一组选项，点“保存”或“发送”后才生效。可以多选；支持半选状态表示“部分子项已选”。Options in a form that apply on Save or Send. Multiple allowed; the mixed state means some children are selected.
开关Switch一项立即生效的设置。拨动后乐观更新，失败时回滚并提示。One setting that applies at once. Updates optimistically; rolls back with a notice on failure.
单选Radio互斥的 2–5 项，全部可见。更多时用下拉选择；两项且是开和关时用开关。Two to five exclusive options, all visible. More: a select. Just on and off: a switch.
选择卡片Choice cards选项需要说明时，用带标题和一句说明的卡片。When options need explaining, use cards with a title and one line.

该做与不该做Do and don’t

该做Do
要求两步验证Require two-step
开关的文字写设置本身，开关状态说明开或关。The label names the setting; the switch shows on or off.
不该做Don’t
关闭两步验证Turn off two-step
不要用否定或动作式的文字，读者会搞不清“开”是什么意思。Don’t write negative or action labels; readers can’t tell what on means.

属性Props

属性Prop类型Type默认Default说明Description
checkedbooleanfalse是否选中。Whether it’s on.
onChange(checked: boolean) => void—切换时回调。Called on toggle.
labelReactNode—文字标签，点击也会切换。Label; clicking it toggles too.
descriptionReactNode—勾选框下方的一行说明。A line below the checkbox label.
disabledbooleanfalse不可用；在说明里写原因。Unavailable; say why in the description.

无障碍Accessibility

点击区域Hit area整行（框和文字）都可以点击，手机上不小于 44px。The whole row toggles; at least 44px on phones.
角色Roles勾选框是原生 checkbox；开关是 role="switch" 和 aria-checked。Checkbox is native; switch uses role="switch" with aria-checked.
