用法Usage

何时用When需要确认、填写少量信息或警告不可撤销的后果时。其余情况用侧面板、页内展开或 Toast。To confirm, collect a little input, or warn about an irreversible result. Otherwise use a side panel, inline expansion or a toast.
文案Copy标题写成问题或动作；主按钮重复标题里的动词：“撤销分享？” → “撤销分享”。Title as a question or action; the primary repeats its verb: Revoke share? → Revoke share.
按钮顺序Button order次要在左、主按钮在右，放在底部右侧。Secondary left, primary right, bottom right.
手机Mobile确认类居中；长表单全屏推入；轻选择改为底部抽屉。Confirmations stay centred; long forms push in full screen; light choices become a bottom sheet.

无障碍Accessibility

焦点Focus打开时焦点进入对话框，Tab 不会离开；关闭后回到触发按钮。Focus moves in on open and stays trapped; it returns to the trigger on close.
EscEsc嵌套时一次 Esc 只关闭最上层。With nesting, one Esc closes only the top layer.
