登记册表格：亚麻表头、细线分隔、需关注的行加底色A register table: linen head, hairline rows, washes on rows that need attention

用于成员、分享、审阅队列这类需要比较的列表。数字右对齐、等宽；手机上每行变成一张卡片。For lists people compare, such as members, shares and review queues. Numbers right-aligned and tabular; each row becomes a card on phones.

用法Usage

何时用When需要按列比较或排序。只是浏览时用列表行或卡片。When people compare or sort by column. For browsing, use list rows or cards.
列Columns首列是标题，可以点击；数字、日期右对齐；最后一列放“更多”。最多 6 列。The first column is the title and is clickable; numbers and dates right-aligned; More in the last column. Six columns at most.
状态Status需关注和错误的行加浅色底；进行中和已完成保持原样，用状态列表达。Attention and error rows take a wash; progress and complete stay plain and use the status column.
空与加载Empty and loading加载时保留表头和 5 行骨架；空表写一句说明和一个动作。While loading keep the head and five skeleton rows; an empty table has one line and one action.
手机Mobile每行变成卡片：首列是标题，其余列合并为一行元信息。Rows become cards: the first column as the title, the rest as one line of metadata.

无障碍Accessibility

语义Semantics用原生 table；可排序表头带 aria-sort。A native table; sortable headers carry aria-sort.
可点击的行Clickable rows行内的标题是真正的链接，键盘可以到达。The title in each row is a real link, reachable by keyboard.
