结构Structure

页头Header标题 + 最多 1 个主按钮和 2 个次按钮。Title with at most one primary and two secondary buttons.
工具栏Toolbar搜索（写明范围）、排序、筛选、视图切换；右侧显示数量。Search (with its scope), sort, filters and the view switch; the count on the right.
选择Selection卡片和行都可以整块点选，Shift 连选，Esc 取消。星标在卡片右上角，不进操作栏。Whole cards and rows select, Shift extends, Esc clears. The star sits top right of a card, not in the bar.
空与加载Empty and loading骨架与最终布局一致；空状态一句话加一个动作。Skeletons match the final layout; empty states have one line and one action.
