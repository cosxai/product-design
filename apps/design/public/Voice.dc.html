直接、有把握、平实Direct, confident, plain

短句。标题就是发现，写成带数字的事实。谈论事务所和工作，不谈论“我们”。Short sentences. The headline is the finding, stated as a fact with the number in it. Speak about the firm and the work, not about “us”.

规则Rules

语域Register直接、有把握、平实。不亲昵，不喊口号。Direct, confident, plain. Never chummy, never a slogan.
人称Person把读者当作事务所来称呼；少用“我们”。Address the reader as the firm; avoid “we”.
大小写Casing标题和正文都用句首大写，不用标题式大写。Sentence case for headlines and body; never title case.
英文English英式拼写。日期 12 August 2026，金额 £1.2m，时间 08:30 to 18:30。British. Dates as 12 August 2026, money as £1.2m, time as 08:30 to 18:30.
数字Numbers每个数字都要有出处，没有出处就删掉。空格写破折号，不写 0。Every figure is sourced or removed. Empty cells take a dash, never a zero.
方法说明Method notes每张图表都有脚注：数据从哪来，以及它不能说明什么。Every graphic says where the data came from and what it does not claim.
从不Never夸张形容词、感叹号、表情符号、总结金句。唯一的装饰是间隔点 · 。Hype adjectives, exclamation marks, emoji, takeaway lines. The middle dot · is the only ornament.
