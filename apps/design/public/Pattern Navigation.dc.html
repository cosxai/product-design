结构Structure

图标栏Icon rail60px，放品牌方块、模块、Agent 和头像。永远可见；模块只按权限显示或隐藏，不显示灰色占位。60px: the brand tile, modules, Agent and the avatar. Always visible; modules show or hide by permission, never as greyed placeholders.
上下文栏Context column232px，放当前模块的视图和最近项。可以收起，偏好会记住；收起时悬停图标会弹出。232px: the module’s views and recent items. Collapsible and remembered; when collapsed, hovering a module shows it as a flyout.
进入实体Entering an entity打开项目、站点这类实体后，上下文栏换成实体菜单：顶部是返回和实体切换器，中间是实体的功能，底部是实体设置。Inside an entity such as a project or site, the column becomes its menu: back and a switcher at the top, its functions in the middle, its settings at the bottom.
两层为限Two levels菜单最多两层。需要第三层时，改成页内标签或筛选。Two menu levels at most. Anything deeper becomes in-page tabs or filters.
面包屑Breadcrumb从工作区品牌开始；超过 4 段时中间折叠。Starts at the workspace brand; more than four segments fold in the middle.
手机Mobile图标栏变为底部 5 个标签；上下文栏变为一页，逐级进入。The rail becomes five bottom tabs; the context column becomes a page you step into.
